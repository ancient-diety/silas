TRIGGERS

Things that can
affect us.

This page contains information about subjects, situations, and forms of content that may cause distress or affect members of our system.

IMPORTANT

Please keep these in mind. Seriously.

Triggers can affect members of our system differently. Something that is harmless to one of us may be deeply upsetting to another, or worse.

01

Gore

This is placed on top because it is the most severe trigger in our system. The members affected by this are Bella, Jinx, the littles, and Chloe. For Bella, gore, extremely bloody visuals, death, etc. can cause her to go into a state of panic. She may freeze up, or become nauseous and even cry depending on the depth of the visual. She can't handle shooting games and more horrors games for this reason and it has gotten to the point where she she has decided to only watch familiar cartoons because she can't handle another panic attack. It's very annoying, and hurtful at times, when people attempt to show this type of violent media to her. -Dylan

For Jinx, being exposed to it makes her extremely angry, it doesnt make her panic like it does for Bella. Chances are, she'll just want to hurt you in any way she can because the anger is so overwhelming.

For the little's it should be obvious why this kind of media can trigger them, they are children, and it would be appreciated if it were never mention around them.

And lastly, Chloe. Gore and Violent media can cause her to cry or start shaking uncontrollably, similarly to Bella.

02

Sexual Media

Certain jokes are fine depending on who you are speaking to, mostly with members like Hex or Ares, because they'll either laugh or deflect.

But a lot of the time, members like Bella and Jinx can get extremely uncomfortable, even if they were the ones who started it, that doesn't mean they'll keep finding it funny or safe. They might feel scared to talk to you if you keep suggestive conversations going, or show them suggestive images.

03

Holes

This is a trigger that affects the entire system.

Any clumps of holes, lines, or unnatural looking patterns close together will make the system panic.

This panic includes; Scratching themselves until they remove skin in certain areas

Shaking uncontrollably

Hyperventilating

Crying

And in rare cases, passing out.

This is one of the harder ones to avoid, but if you are aware of it, DO NOT go out of your way to trigger the system with it.

It is not funny.
