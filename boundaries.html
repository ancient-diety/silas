OUR BOUNDARIES

Please keep these in mind.

This page will focus on our boundaries as a system, not individual.

01

General respect

Don't ask for trauma details

Just because we struggle with oversharing doesn't mean we're comfortable discussing certain experiences. If it happens, it happens, but we will close off if you ask.

Don't compare splits

We're different people with different personalities. Comparisons aren't helpful.

Don't call us characters

We're real people sharing one body. Calling us characters feels invalidating.

Don't assume we're all Bella

Even if some of us communicate similarly because of masking, we aren't all Bella.

02

Fronting & identity

Don't try to trigger switches

It can be distressing and disorienting. Please let switching happen naturally.

Don't try to aggrivate or annoy us if you know an anger holder is in front.

It's not funny and can cause discomfort for the whole system, not just for the anger holder. They are people too.

Don't sexualise the sexual trauma holders

You will end up blocked, and we'll struggle trying to feel like we're worth anything for a while.

03

Personal boundaries

Don't force us to show our face or talk on call/voice note

We struggle enough with social interaction, we don't need the pressure on top of it.

Don't fake claim us

We understand it can be unbelievable, that doesn't mean you can make us feel like a dumb liar.

04

Sensitive topics

DO NOT mention our eating habits.

It's something we struggle with a lot and triggering that can lead us to starving ourselves.

Do not ask us to take part in or watch anything gorey

We will panic. It's not just a light joke. Just because it doesn't phase you doesn't mean we aren't affected.

05

Interacting with us

Be mindful of your words

We're okay with jokes sometimes but it's seriously important to think before you talk to us. We can take things the wrong way and get hurt easily.

Don't make us the laughing stock

Jokes are only funny if everyone is laughing. And we can't handle being the one laughed at.

IF YOU'RE UNSURE

Ask us.

We're open to questions, and obviously not every single boundary is on the page, only the key ones. So please, ask if you aren't sure.
